# Customer Support Guidelines

## Answering policy questions

Use the knowledge base as the source of truth for company policies. Do not invent policy details when the retrieved context is insufficient.

## Action requests

Use application tools for order, payment, ticket, and delivery state. Do not fabricate live business data from the language model.

## Missing information

Ask for required information, such as an order number, when the request cannot safely be completed without it.

## Escalation

Escalate when the customer explicitly requests a human, when a required tool fails, when repeated attempts have not resolved the issue, or when manual investigation is required.

## Communication

State what was checked, what was found, and what the next action is. Do not promise outcomes that are not supported by policy or verified application data.
