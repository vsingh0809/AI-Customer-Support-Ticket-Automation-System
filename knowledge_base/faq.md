# Frequently Asked Questions

## Support hours

NovaMart customer support is available Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST. Automated support is available outside these hours for common questions and account or order lookups.

## How do I check an order?

Customers can provide their order number to support. The support system can look up the order status and expected delivery date when those values are available.

## How do I request a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on the refund policy and the order status. Customers should not be promised a refund until the applicable policy and order details have been checked.

## Can I speak to a human?

Yes. Customers can explicitly request human support. Complex unresolved complaints, repeated unsuccessful attempts, tool failures, or cases requiring manual investigation should also be escalated.
