# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

A customer may request a refund within 7 calendar days of delivery for eligible products that are unused and in resalable condition, subject to applicable exclusions.

## Damaged or incorrect items

Customers who receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item should contact support as soon as possible. Support may request photographs or other evidence and may create a ticket for manual review.

## Refund processing

When a refund is approved, NovaMart initiates the refund to the original payment method. The final posting time depends on the payment provider and is not guaranteed by NovaMart.

## Important support rule

Support must not promise a specific refund amount or settlement time until the order and payment details have been checked.
