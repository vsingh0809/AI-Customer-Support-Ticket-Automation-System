# Payment Policy

## Payment status

A payment may be recorded as pending, paid, failed, or refunded. The payment system is the source of truth for transaction-specific status.

## Payment deducted but order failed

When a customer reports that payment was deducted but an order failed, support should check both the order and payment records. The customer should not be promised an immediate refund without verifying the transaction state.

## Duplicate charge concerns

Possible duplicate charges require payment verification. Create a support ticket when transaction records do not fully explain the customer's report.
