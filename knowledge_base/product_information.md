# Product Information

## Wireless Headphones

NovaMart Wireless Headphones are an over-ear Bluetooth product intended for everyday audio use.

- Battery life: up to 30 hours under standard usage conditions.
- Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.x compatible devices.
- In-box items: headphones, USB charging cable, and quick-start guide.
- Warranty: 12 months from the purchase date for manufacturing defects, subject to warranty terms.

Product availability and order-specific delivery information must be checked through the order system rather than inferred from this document.
