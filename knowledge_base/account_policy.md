# Account Policy

## Account information

Customers should use their authenticated account to access account-specific information.

## Personal data

Support should only expose customer information that the authenticated customer is authorized to access.

## Account recovery

Account recovery requests that cannot be completed through the normal flow should be escalated to human support.
